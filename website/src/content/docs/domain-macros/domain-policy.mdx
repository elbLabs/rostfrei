# `domain_policy`

Make policy explicit and reusable. A policy is a singular trait with arbitrary Rust inputs and a closed outcome vocabulary when the domain needs one. Keep the policy signature independent of infrastructure.

- Aggregate or entity implementation
- No owner metadata
- No projection group

**Placement:** [Aggregate policy](/docs/project-structure/aggregate-policy), [Entity policy](/docs/project-structure/entity-policy), or [value-object policy](/docs/project-structure/value-object-policy) — `policy.rs`.
