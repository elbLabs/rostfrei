# `domain_decision`

Make policy explicit and reusable. A decision is a singular trait with arbitrary Rust inputs and a closed outcome vocabulary when the domain needs one. Keep the policy signature independent of infrastructure.

- Aggregate or entity implementation
- No owner metadata
- No projection group

**Placement:** [Aggregate decision](/docs/project-structure/aggregate-decision) or [value-object decision](/docs/project-structure/value-object-decision) — `decision.rs`.
